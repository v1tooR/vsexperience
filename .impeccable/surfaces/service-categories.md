# Service categories

Scope: extension of the existing service-page family, `/servicos/`, seven category routes, their English equivalents and the category links on both homepages. Visitor mode: Persuade. Scope explicitly excludes new individual service pages and adopting prices from Produtos-Variados.md.

Audience and task: prospective clients choose the right category, inspect the included specialities and discuss an appropriate scope. All 64 source services are included; existing e-commerce and systems/SaaS remain accessible. Existing VS Social is related from Branding and Education.

WORLD: inherit `servicos/DESIGN.md`, its linen/graphite/carmine fields, locally hosted Roboto Flex and Plus Jakarta Sans, variable service specimens, fine rules, tabs and curtain shader. The user explicitly pinned the incumbent design system; no identity or concept selection is unresolved.

STORY: recognise a category, explore a concrete example, inspect specialities, understand the process and initiate a contextual WhatsApp conversation. Real cases support only documented work; authored examples are explicitly demonstrations. Education availability is on enquiry. Existing public pricing remains unchanged.

FIRST VIEWPORT: existing full-width variable category specimen, then the service family's copy/action and demonstration composition. Website and product categories show real case mockups with contextual captions; Branding has a brand specimen, AI a workflow, Consulting a diagnosis/priority/roadmap report and Education a learning workbook. Tabs operate by keyboard and pointer; an explicit pause control stops autoplay.

FORM: extension of the already chosen VS service composition, not an open replacement surface. Seed key: not applicable, user-pinned incumbent system. Each category varies the authored material inside the inherited composition rather than inventing a new visual world.

Signature interaction: the category specimen responds to pointer weight/width; demonstrations switch between meaningful stages using inherited accessible tabs, with pause, focus/hover suspension and reduced-motion handling. AI workflow animation is bounded by viewport visibility. All catalogue content is static HTML with native disclosures and contextual links.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS on Apache; shared source generates category content in PT/EN; preserve current service routes and prices. No invented case metrics, client work, course availability or product launch status. No new individual service routes in this task.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
