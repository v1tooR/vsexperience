# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The proposal is read by the FutRanking decision maker. The product it presents will serve three audiences: platform administrators, team managers, and the public that follows matches, rankings, teams, and players.

## Product Purpose

Present and approve the development of the FutRanking MVP: a responsive football competition platform where teams manage squads, administrators register match results and events, and the public follows statistics and rankings.

## Positioning

FutRanking turns every registered match into an organized, transparent, and easy-to-follow competition history.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: approve the project through WhatsApp.
- Secondary CTA: review the product prototype and scope.
- The line to remember: Every match leaves a ranking.
- Belief ladder: the competition needs one reliable source; each role receives the right level of access; results update the standings automatically; the MVP covers the complete journey from roster to ranking.
- Proof on hand: a functional interface concept embedded in the proposal, with realistic admin, team, and public ranking states.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, precise, and energetic. The voice should feel like a well-run championship control room: direct enough for administrators, exciting enough for teams, and clear enough for the public.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic black-and-neon-green sports templates, betting-site language, visual noise, fake live-data claims, and dashboard grids made from indistinguishable cards.

## Design Principles

- Show the operating flow, not just a feature list.
- Make permissions and responsibilities immediately understandable.
- Treat score, position, and performance as the product's visual language.
- Keep the commercial proposal recognizably VS Experience while giving FutRanking its own competition identity.
- Present the first release honestly as a focused MVP.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive web interface with semantic structure, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, touch targets of at least 44px, and status communication that does not depend on color alone.
