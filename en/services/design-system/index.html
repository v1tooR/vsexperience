A language shared by the whole team.

A design system is the set of rules and reusable pieces that define how your product looks and behaves. Instead of redesigning buttons and forms on every screen, the team assembles interfaces from pieces that have already been tested.

Visible consistency

The same language on the website, the system and the app, reinforcing the brand on every screen.

Delivery speed

New screens ship faster, with less rework between design and development.

Accessibility by default

Contrast, focus and states defined once and inherited by every component.

Continuity between design and code

Names and rules aligned in Figma and in code to make implementation and maintenance easier.

From token to screen, a single source of truth.

A color or spacing change made in the token reaches every component and screen that uses it. That's what keeps a product consistent as it grows.

Tokens

FoundationColor, typography, space, radius, shadow and motion, with clear names.

Components

PiecesButton, field, select, card, modal and toast, each with variants and states.

Patterns

CombinationsForms, tables, navigation, feedback and empty states solved once.

Screens

ProductDashboard, sign-up, checkout and onboarding assembled from the pieces above.

Change the token at the top and the change flows down, on its own, to the last screen.

When a design system is the right choice.

It makes sense when

  • Your digital product has many screens and more than one person designing or developing.
  • The interface has become inconsistent and every new screen creates rework.
  • You'll launch new modules, products or versions in the coming months.
  • Several brands or products need to share the same foundation.

Another path works better when

  • Only a few pages: the basic component system already comes with the Company Website.
  • The brand doesn't exist yet: logo and identity come before the system, starting at $450.
  • The product hasn't been built yet: start with MVP, which already includes the visual foundation.

Design system FAQ.

How much does a design system cost?

A typical project, with about 25 components in Figma and code, comes to around $1,200. Foundation ranges from $540 to $810; Product system, from $1,100 to $1,600; and the ongoing partnership costs from $400 to $680 per month.

Is a design system only for large companies?

No. A small product with a well-built foundation grows without piling up inconsistencies. The Foundation tier exists precisely for those just starting out who want to avoid rework later.

Do you deliver in code or only in Figma?

Both are possible. Foundation is delivered in Figma; Product system includes the components in code (React and CSS), with the same names and tokens as in Figma.

Can it be built from an existing product?

Yes, and that's the most common case. The audit starts from the current screens, keeps what works and consolidates what's duplicated, without requiring the product to be rebuilt from scratch.

How is it different from a brand guide?

A brand guide defines the logo, colors and tone of voice. A design system turns that into ready-to-use interface pieces, with states, behavior rules and code.

Shall we organize the foundation for your product to grow?

Tell me the size of the product, who designs and who develops. The conversation helps identify inconsistencies and the component scope to estimate the work.
