When a custom system is the right choice.

It makes sense when

  • A repetitive process lives in spreadsheets and depends on someone's memory.
  • Several people, with different roles, need to access the same data.
  • You have a product idea and want to validate it with real users.
  • Your routine needs to connect with WhatsApp, payments or an ERP without rework.

Another path works better when

  • An off-the-shelf tool solves almost everything for a low monthly fee. Discovery will tell you that honestly.
  • The goal is to present the company and generate leads: see Company Website.
  • What you need is to sell products online: see E-commerce.

Custom software FAQ.

How much does custom software cost?

The average investment is $1,250, with projects between $540 and $2,150. Internal tools range from $540 to $900; management systems and CRMs, from $1,100 to $1,600; and SaaS platforms start at $1,800. The final price comes from discovery, module by module.

What is an MVP?

It's the smallest version of the product that solves the core problem and can already be used for real. It validates the idea with real users before investing in every feature.

How is the price defined?

After discovery, with the scope split into modules. Each module has an estimated effort, and you decide what goes into the first version and what comes later.

Do I own the data?

Yes. The database runs on infrastructure contracted in your company's name. Code ownership and license terms are defined in the contract before work starts.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Every system is responsive and can be installed as an app (PWA) without going through the app stores. Publishing on the App Store and Google Play is quoted separately.

What problem does your next system need to solve?

Tell me where the routine gets stuck, who uses the process and what needs to improve. We define the scope of the first version and the priorities to estimate the investment.
