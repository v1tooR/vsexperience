Every section brings the visitor closer to a decision.

A landing page is a page focused on one offer and one action: request a quote, book, sign up or buy. The structure presents the value, answers questions and makes the next step easy to find.

Strategy before layout

I learn the offer, the audience and the questions that need answers before creating the layout.

Conversion copy

Copy that explains your offer clearly and builds the argument up to the call to action.

Custom, responsive design

A layout created for your brand, with careful reading and navigation on mobile and desktop.

Ready to measure

WhatsApp, form, Analytics and pixel set up at delivery.

A narrative that follows the decision.

The order of the sections follows the visitor's questions: what you offer, why trust you, how it works and what to do now.

  1. Attention

    Hero

    A clear promise, quick proof and the first call to action, all visible without scrolling.

  2. Interest

    Problem

    The visitor's situation described in their own words, so they recognize themselves.

  3. Interest

    Solution

    How the service solves it, in steps that are easy to understand and picture.

  4. Desire

    Social proof

    Testimonials and real cases that back up the arguments and help the visitor evaluate your offer.

  5. Desire

    Offer

    What's included, terms, guarantees and what sets you apart from the competition.

  6. Action

    Objections

    FAQs that answer whatever is still holding the decision back.

  7. Action

    Final CTA

    WhatsApp or form front and center, without distractions, for those who made it to the end.

When a landing page is the right choice.

It makes sense when

  • You have one main offer and want to smooth the path from campaign to contact.
  • You will invest in ads and need a page that makes the most of every paid click.
  • You are launching a product, service, event or campaign with a set date.
  • You need to go live fast, with a lean investment and measurable results.

Another format works better when

  • Your company has several services that deserve their own pages: see Company Website.
  • You want to sell products with a cart and online payment: see E-commerce.
  • What you need is a tool with login and data: see Systems & SaaS.

Landing page FAQ.

How much does a landing page cost?

The average investment is $230, with projects between $140 and $320. The Essential tier goes from $140 to $200; the Strategic tier, with copy, analytics and technical SEO, goes from $220 to $290; and Campaign starts at $320. The final price is set after discovery.

Do I need to have the copy ready?

No. In the Strategic tier, copywriting is included: the copy is written from the discovery and sent for your approval before design. In Essential, you send the content and it is reviewed and organized on the page.

Does a landing page work without ads?

Yes. It can receive visits from Instagram, your bio link, Google, referrals or campaigns. Results depend on the offer, the audience and the quality of that traffic. The page organizes the message and makes the desired action easy.

Can I edit the page later?

Yes. Small changes are covered by the six-month maintenance plan and larger changes are quoted separately. When the page is built on WordPress, you can also edit text and images directly in the dashboard.

How long until the page is live?

Between 10 and 15 business days after the materials are sent. What affects the timeline most is how quickly each step is approved.

Shall we give your offer a path to action?

Tell me about your offer, your audience and the action the page needs to drive. From there, we align the structure and scope to define the quote.
