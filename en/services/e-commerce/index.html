The shopping experience and the operation working together.

E-commerce is the complete online store: products, stock, payment, shipping, orders and customers working together. The design drives the purchase, and the structure behind it supports day-to-day operations.

A clear shopping journey

Search, categories, product page and cart designed to reduce abandonment.

Checkout with instant payment, card and bank slip

Payment set up, shipping calculation and discount coupons working from day one.

Integrated with the operation

Bling, Mercado Livre, Correios and Melhor Envio connected as needed (Brazilian services; equivalents for other markets are defined in discovery).

Dashboard and training

Your team learns to add products, track orders and publish promotions from the dashboard.

The whole journey, from first click to after-sales.

When e-commerce is the right choice.

It makes sense when

  • You sell products and want your own channel, with your brand and your rules.
  • You depend on marketplaces and want to reduce the weight of fees on revenue.
  • You serve both consumers and retailers, with different prices and minimums.
  • You need to centralize stock and orders from several channels in one place.

Another format works better when

  • You have few items and sell better through WhatsApp conversations: see Landing Page.
  • The catalog exists to present, not to sell online: see Company Website.
  • The operation has very specific rules, such as subscriptions or its own marketplace: see Systems & SaaS.

E-commerce FAQ.

How much does an e-commerce store cost?

The average investment is $670. The essential store, with catalog, checkout, Pix, card and shipping, ranges from $500 to $810; the store integrated with Bling and Mercado Livre, from $810 to $1,600; and advanced operations start at $1,800. Payment, shipping and hosting fees are paid separately.

Which platform do you use?

In most projects, WordPress with WooCommerce: no platform subscription, a familiar dashboard and many available integrations. Operations with very specific rules may call for a custom solution, defined during discovery.

Are payment and shipping fees included?

No. Gateway fees, shipping, hosting and ERP are contracted in your company's name and paid directly to the providers. The project price covers development and setup.

Who adds the products?

Delivery includes the initial product setup and training so your team can carry on. Large catalogs can be imported via spreadsheet, priced by volume.

Can I sell on a marketplace and in my own store at the same time?

Yes. With the Bling integration, stock, orders and products can be synced between your own store and the marketplace. The setup takes your operation's channels, rules and products into account.

Shall we build a store that makes sense for your operation?

Tell me what you sell, on which channels and how you manage stock and orders. This conversation guides the platform, the integrations and the scope of the quote.
